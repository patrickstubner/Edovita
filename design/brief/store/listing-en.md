**Name:** Edovita

## Short description

Track meals and goals on-device. Built for clarity and care.

## Full description

Edovita helps you log meals, calories and protein with goals to lose, gain or maintain weight. Phase 1 is **local-first**: health data stays on your device.

Planned features (prototype reference): today view, recipes and shopping list, progress, body metrics, weekly review, help resources, light/dark mode.

Edovita is not medical or dietetic advice. Allergen filters are hints, not guarantees.

Primary colour: #0B7F6B.
